## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an approachable, luminous, and positive productivity experience. Drawing directly from modern clean mobile operating software and friendly AI-native workflow companions, it replaces intimidating task management interfaces with soft warmth, optimistic pastel accents, and effortless 1-tap navigation.

### Emotional Tone & Philosophy
- **Gentle Encouragement & Zero-Shame Micro-copy:** The interface embraces the user with welcoming greetings, lightweight completion metrics, and supportive feedback loops rather than clinical task backlogs.
- **Luminous Airiness:** Crisp white cards float seamlessly above warm neutral canvas tones, utilizing gentle ambient drops and pastel category containers to bring visual delight.
- **Effortless Tactility:** Chunky, pill-shaped action zones and squircle badge containers invite direct touch, reducing cognitive friction for rapid daily prioritization.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Influences
- **Soft Modern Human Interface:** Pill-shaped navigation islands, rounded segment controls, and generous tap targets.
- **Pastel Color Chunking:** Clean categorical blocks—airy lilac, mint mist, warm peach, and powder azure—organize complex schedules into instantly scannable, bite-sized moments.
- **Subtle Dimensionality:** Extra-diffused, multi-stop drop shadows coupled with faint hairline borders evoke tactile, card-deck modularity.

## Colors

The color system delivers high legibility through light-mode vibrancy. Clean white container cards sit on an ultra-soft cool-grey canvas (`#F8FAFC`), while primary interactions are powered by an electric soft violet (`#635BFF`).

### Functional Palette Architecture
- **Primary Violet (`#635BFF`):** Powers key conversion buttons, floating creation action buttons, active navigation states, and primary analytics graphs.
- **Mint Vitality (`#10B981`):** Applied to progress status tracks, completion rings, streak badges, and supportive positive feedback loops.
- **Warm Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Dedicated to high-priority flags, pending alerts, and calendar tags.
- **Categorical Pastel Washes:** Softened, desaturated background tints provide low-cognitive-load organizational buckets:
  - `surface-tint-violet` (`#F2F0FF`): General tasks, focus reviews.
  - `surface-tint-blue` (`#EDF5FF`): Total focus time, ongoing deep work sessions.
  - `surface-tint-rose` (`#FFF0F0`): Meetings, high-priority notifications.
  - `surface-tint-mint` (`#ECFDF5`): Weekly overall progress, accomplished cycles.

### Contrast & Content Tiers
All dark typography strictly avoids raw pure black (`#000000`). Headlines utilize rich slate (`#111827`) achieving 14.5:1 contrast against pure white cards, while secondary descriptors and supportive labels rely on `#64748B` and `#94A3B8` respectively for clean structural hierarchy.

## Typography

The typography is built entirely on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, providing clean modern geometric clarity paired with friendly humanist terminals.

### Typographic Rationale
- **Friendly Authority:** Rounded apertures and balanced letterforms deliver an approachable, non-intimidating aesthetic while maintaining razor-sharp legibility at tiny badge scale.
- **Metric Prominence:** Quantitative focus statistics (`stat-metric`) receive heavy weights (`700`) and optical letter-spacing condensation (`-0.03em`) to anchor daily overview cards and analytical graphs.
- **Tabular Figures for Timers & Schedules:** Dynamic numeric components (hours, percentages, timestamps) enforce tabular numbers to maintain rock-solid horizontal layout balance.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8-point rhythmic grid, emphasizing lightweight density and immediate touch targets.

### Screen Adaptability & Fluid Grid
- **Mobile Handheld (< 640px):** Single-column workflow with `margin-sm: 1rem` and `gutter-sm: 0.75rem`. Features an elevated bottom dock navigation bar providing thumb-zone access to core functional pillars (Home, Tasks, Create, Calendar, Profile).
- **Tablet (640px – 1024px):** 6-column grid with `margin: 1.5rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Switches to a dual-pane view: overview analytics on the leading pane, task schedule timeline on the trailing pane.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout max-width constrained to `1200px`, anchored with a persistent left pill navigation rail and modular dashboard widgets.

### Spatial Flow
- Cards within a dashboard row space at `space-md` (16px), keeping content dense yet scannable.
- Sub-item task lists space at `space-sm` (8px), maintaining clean separation while allowing multi-item grouping on smaller screens.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through gentle illumination rather than heavy drops, creating an airy sense of weightlessness.

### Surface Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Ground layer finished in soft matte cool slate (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Level 1 (Modular Surface Cards):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces featuring an ultra-soft ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(100, 116, 139, 0.06)` combined with a microscopic crisp boundary `1px solid #EEF2F6`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Docks & Modals):** Bottom navigation docks and action sheets float above the canvas with a multi-layered cloud shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(99, 91, 255, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Primary Action Floating Island):** Dedicated elevated floating action button (`+`) featuring a soft directional primary glow: `0 8px 16px -2px rgba(99, 91, 255, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a high roundedness standard (`3` - Pill-shaped), providing friendly, safe, and fluid contours throughout all UI modules.

### Shape Hierarchy
- **Pill Navigations & Filter Chips:** Pure circular capsules (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) provide tactile touch comfort.
- **Overview & Task Cards:** Styled with `rounded-2xl` (1.25rem / 20px) to give information modules a soft, friendly pebble appearance.
- **Micro Badges & Icon Backdrops:** Squircle shape language (`rounded-xl` / 12px) creates comfortable nesting inside larger parent containers.
- **Action Buttons & Form Inputs:** Fully pill-shaped or smooth rounded-xl elements to maximize tap surface affordance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill:** Background in `#635BFF` with crisp `#FFFFFF` typography (`label-lg`), height `48px`, horizontal padding `24px`, fully rounded (`rounded-full`). Shadow: `0 8px 16px -2px rgba(99, 91, 255, 0.35)`. Hover: 4% lightness lift and gentle scale up (`1.02`). Active: scale `0.98`.
- **Floating Action Center Button (+):** Violet `#635BFF` circle (`48px x 48px`), optic white centered icon, elevated above the bottom navigation island.
- **Secondary / Soft Tint:** Background in `surface-tint-violet` (`#F2F0FF`), text `#635BFF`, no border, `rounded-full`.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background, text `#64748B`, transitioning to `#111827` on interaction.

### Overview Metric Cards
- Container styled in clean `#FFFFFF` with `rounded-2xl` and internal padding `space-md` (16px).
- Internal layout hosts a top row with a tinted squircle icon backdrop (`32px x 32px`, `rounded-xl`) and a category title (`label-sm`).
- Center features large quantitative typography (`stat-metric`) alongside a mini descriptive progress indicator (`body-sm`).

### Tasks & List Rows
- Horizontal card item in `#FFFFFF` with `rounded-2xl` and hairline outline (`1px solid #EEF2F6`).
- Left cluster: Squircle category icon holder (`36px x 36px`) colored in contextual category wash (e.g., `#FFF0F0` for urgent meetings, `#F2F0FF` for creative tasks).
- Center cluster: Task label (`headline-md`) stacked above inline priority chip and horizontal linear progress track (`4px` height with rounded caps).
- Right cluster: Interactive circular completion toggle or pill tag displaying relative countdown (`in 45 min`).

### Chips & Segment Controls
- **Filter Tabs (All, To Do, In Progress, Done):** Segment track contained inside a soft `#F1F5F9` pill rail. Active tab fills in solid `#635BFF` with white text; inactive tabs rest in `#64748B` with smooth spring transitions between selection shifts.
- **Priority Indicator Chips:** Compact pills (`height: 22px`, `padding: 2px 8px`) displaying semantic colored dots (`6px`) paired with mini uppercase label (`label-sm`).

### Checkboxes & Completion Targets
- Circular checkbox control (`22px x 22px`), border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`.
- Checked state: Smooth morph fill to `#635BFF` or `#10B981` revealing a centered white vector checkmark glyph with subtle scale bounce.

### Bottom Navigation Bar Island
- Floating pill-shaped dock anchored at screen bottom (`height: 64px`, `margin: 0 16px 16px 16px`).
- Background: `#FFFFFF` with frosted glass backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`).
- Five ergonomic zones: 4 tab icon-labels (`Home`, `Tasks`, `Calendar`, `Profile`) flanked symmetrically around the center raised primary action button (`+`).

### AI Assistant Floating Bar
- Soft rounded pill container (`rounded-full`) with a gentle gradient border (`#635BFF` to `#38BDF8`).
- Houses a friendly prompt placeholder (`"Ask AI Assistant..."`), a sparkling gem glyph, and a trailing circular arrow submit button.
